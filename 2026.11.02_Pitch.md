# PITCH

## INTRO
It's your 16th birthday, and you're so excited that your parents gave you a new phone as a present. Everyone in your class has been on the Zine, a cool new app that your old phone didn't support. With your brand new tech in hand, you log in and find that the app is about you.

## INSPIRATION
- Teen magazine activities
- Y2K aesthetic but not really

## GAME REFERENCES
### A Normal Lost Phone
Players find a lost phone and must progressively unlock apps to unveil the story of a trans teenager who left to reinvent herself in a new town.

### Papers, Please
Dystopian game where player is a border officer for Arstotzka and must approve or deny people's entry. It gets progressively harder to tell who should be approved or denied.

### Hypnospace Outlaw
Players enter into the hypnospace, the web that you can browse while you're asleep, as an enforcer. Players seek to report instances of scams, viruses, and cyberbullying, while uncovering the truth about the company that makes hypnospace.
